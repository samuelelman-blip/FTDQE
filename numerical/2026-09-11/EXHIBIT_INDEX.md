## Evidence classes

- **feasibility:** A constraint residual, fine-grid evaluation or stated analytic remainder bound for one returned vector; exact scope and arithmetic are retained.
- **optimality_or_support_minimality:** LP dual/solver records, incumbent/objective gaps, or an infeasible predecessor within a specified nested support family; distinct from feasibility and continuum optimality.
- **declared_scores:** Evaluation/enumeration of the stipulated compiler and conditional state-score functions; distinct from dynamical preparation validation.
- **dynamical_validation:** Hamiltonian/jump-linked trajectories or stationary states with trace, Hermiticity, positivity, residual, integration or independent-method diagnostics.
- **identity:** Hashes identify exact files and detect byte changes; they do not independently validate their numerical content.
